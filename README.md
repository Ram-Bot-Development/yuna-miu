## 🌸 About Yuna Miu

**Yuna Miu** is an upcoming free and open-source Discord bot designed to bring fun, friendships, and personality to Discord communities!

She's a cheerful, mischievous catgirl who loves gaming, making new friends, and creating unforgettable moments.

Yuna is part of the same bot friend group as **RamBot**, but she's an independent project with her own identity, features, and personality.

> "Miu~! Hiii everyone!! 🐾💕 I'm Yuna! Are you ready to have some fun? Nya~! ✨"
